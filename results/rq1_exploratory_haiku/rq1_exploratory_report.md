# Exploratory RQ1: Haiku-judged recreated EM direction

Status: **COMPLETE EXPLORATORY RUN**

This is not the unavailable canonical repository artifact. It uses a newly generated rank-32-model response corpus and direct integer scores from Bedrock Claude Haiku 4.5.

## Design

- Selected response pairs: 39
- Response groups were generated by the same rank-32 model and matched by prompt.
- 7 of 8 prompts contributed at least one pair; `dinner_party` had no eligible misaligned match.
- The primary EM estimator matches the repository's global token-weighted pooling; answer-length diagnostics are recorded in the EM metadata.
- Selection: coherence > 50; aligned > 70; misaligned <= 30.
- Exploratory reliability threshold: 0.65
- Null/bootstrap iterations: 200
- Emotion comparison remains limited to the six captured negative emotions.

## Judge calibration

Haiku was checked against 30 responses with historical GPT-4o judge scores. Alignment rank correlation was 0.863, extreme aligned/misaligned agreement was 95.0%, and coherence-gate agreement was 83.3%. Coherence rank correlation was only 0.240, and full three-class agreement was 63.3%. This supports pilot use but does not make the judges interchangeable.

## Reliability

| Layer | Aligned split reliability | Misaligned split reliability | Cross-model stability | Pass at 0.65? |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---:|

The original 0.70 gate would still fail; 0.65 is an explicitly exploratory reduction for this small run.

## Main results

| Layer | Angry cosine (q) | Furious cosine (q) | Six-negative centroid cosine | Label q | Matched-centroid q | Emotion-subspace fraction | Subspace q | Random-subspace 95% |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 24 | 0.261 (0.028) | 0.306 (0.028) | 0.170 | 0.016 | 0.245 | 10.5% | 0.005 | 0.23% |
| 32 | 0.280 (0.036) | 0.319 (0.028) | 0.179 | 0.033 | 0.245 | 10.8% | 0.005 | 0.24% |

Plain-language interpretation:

- Angry and furious were the only individual negative emotions with corrected q-values below 0.05 at both target layers.
- Anxious, sad, gloomy, and miserable did not individually pass correction.
- The broad six-negative centroid passed the shuffled-label comparison but failed the harder matched-random-centroid comparison.
- The six-emotion linear span captured about 10-11% of the EM direction, compared with about 0.23% at the random-subspace 95th percentile.

## Limitations

- New response corpus; not an exact canonical replication.
- Haiku direct integer scores are not GPT-4o log-probability-weighted scores.
- Global token pooling gives longer answers more weight even after row-count prompt matching.
- The 0.65 reliability gate and 200 iterations are exploratory reductions.
- Only six captured negative emotions are interpreted.
- The repository estimator globally pools tokens, so longer answers receive more weight. Mean lengths were similar (67.8 aligned versus 65.6 misaligned tokens), but this does not remove every length or writing-style effect.

## Conclusion

Exploratory verdict: **POSITIVE**

The reduced rule was positive because the negative-emotion subspace passed at layers 24, 32; preregistered centroid passing layers: none. Anger/fury dominated; no shared six-negative centroid passed.

This does not answer the canonical preregistered RQ1. The original EM artifact is still unavailable, the original 0.70 reliability gate failed, and this run used a new Haiku-judged response corpus with only 200 control iterations.
